Emergency Leak Repairs

Rapid isolation and repair of leaking copper, CPVC, and PEX water lines, slab leaks, pinhole ruptures, and shut-off valves.

Water Heater Services

Installation, maintenance, and element repairs for electric and gas water heaters, as well as high-efficiency tankless units.

  • Tankless unit installation
  • Thermostat & anode replacements
  • Expansion tank safety setups

Drain & Sewer Clearing

Heavy-duty mechanical snaking and clearing of stubborn blockages in kitchen sinks, bathtubs, main sewer lines, and toilets.

  • Main line sewer snake clearing

Fixtures & Faucets
